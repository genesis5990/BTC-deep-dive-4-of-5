The UTXO Mental Model (Recap)

Class 3 established the single most important idea in Bitcoin tracing:

Bitcoin has no accounts and no balances on-chain. It has only coins — Unspent Transaction Outputs (UTXOs).
  • Every UTXO is locked to a locking script (scriptPubKey) — usually "whoever can sign for address X".
  • Every transaction destroys one or more UTXOs entirely as inputs, and creates brand-new UTXOs as outputs.
  • Every satoshi of every input must go somewhere: recipient outputs, a change output back to the sender, or the miner fee.
  • UTXOs can only be spent once, whole, by the holder of the key. Value is never "moved and left behind" — it is always re-minted forward.

Why this makes tracing possible

Because inputs must be destroyed whole, value on a blockchain cannot teleport. Every satoshi arrives at its current location through an unbroken, public, timestamped chain of transactions. The ledger is complete back to the coinbase of every coin. That completeness is the investigator's best friend: if you can find one transaction tied to your subject, you can in principle follow the value forward and backward across the entire history.

60-second warm-up (ask the room)

  1. A suspect's wallet shows "balance 2.4 BTC". What does the blockchain actually store? (Answer: a set of UTXOs summing to 2.4 BTC — the "balance" is a wallet-computed view.)
  2. A transaction has 2 inputs and 3 outputs. One output goes to a merchant. What's a plausible role for each remaining output? (Change to sender + possibly an OP_RETURN/data output, or a second payment.)
  3. Can a spent UTXO ever be spent again? (No — double-spend is exactly what consensus prevents.)

The tracing mindset you will use all hour

Find ANY tx linked to subject→ Identify outputs (payment? change? data?)→ Follow the UTXOs forward→ Cluster addresses via heuristics→ Hit a service (exchange/merchant/mixer)→ Legal process for identity
Reality check: the chain gives you addresses and flows, never names. Identity comes from off-chain points: exchanges, merchants, ISP logs, wallet backups, statements. Your trace tells you where to subpoena.
