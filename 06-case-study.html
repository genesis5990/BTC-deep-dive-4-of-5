Debrief questions for the room (5 min)

  1. Where exactly does on-chain work stop and police work begin? (At E1. The chain proves the deposit; the platform's records provide the identity.)
  2. Which single artifact most strongly links the chain to the ransom note? (The OP_RETURN payload — value never moved through it, but data did.)
  3. What breaks this case if the defense hires an expert? (Overclaimed heuristics — e.g., asserting H2a/H2b ownership without the single-signer evidence, or clustering a CoinJoin.)
  4. What do you request in legal process? (KYC for E1 deposit, withdrawals post-T7, IP/session logs, and — if available — the OP_RETURN-tag association with any account notes.)
The one-sentence takeaway: follow the change spine, decode the data outputs, stop at the service door — then let legal process turn an address into a name.
